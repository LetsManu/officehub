# OfficeHub – user guide

OfficeHub is our employee portal. Here you can ask for vacation, get expenses paid back, order
equipment, find company documents and read the latest news – all in one place.

You do not need any technical knowledge. If something is unclear, the HR team is happy to help.

---

## Signing in

1. Open OfficeHub in your browser (your HR team will tell you the address).
2. Enter your email address and password, then click **Sign in**.
3. Forgot your password? Please contact the HR team – they can set a new one for you.

If you switched on two-step sign-in, OfficeHub then asks for the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.
Lost your phone? Enter one of your recovery codes instead, or ask the admin team to reset two-step sign-in.

You can switch between **Deutsch** and **English** at the bottom of the sign-in page.
After signing in, OfficeHub uses the language you chose in your profile.

To sign out, click **Sign out** at the bottom of the menu.

**Install as an app:** in Chrome or Edge click **Install as app** in the menu (on phones: *Add to home screen*).
OfficeHub then opens in its own window like a normal program.

## The home page

The home page is your starting page. It shows:

- **Important** – the pinned announcement, if there is one.
- **At a glance** – your open requests, room bookings and visitors today (and approvals waiting, for managers).
- **Quick actions** – the things people do most often, for example request vacation or submit an expense.
- **Needs your attention** – things only you can do, such as preparing your employee review or deciding on requests.
- **Recent activity** – the latest news about your requests, visitors and reviews.
- **My open requests**, **latest announcements**, **your room bookings today** and **upcoming events**.

### Search

Click **Search** at the top (or press **Ctrl+K**, on a Mac **⌘K**) and type what you are looking for: a colleague,
a document, an announcement, one of your requests or a tool. Use the arrow keys and Enter, or just click a result.

### Notifications

The bell at the top shows a number when something new happened – for example when your request was approved,
someone commented, or your visitor arrived. Click a notification to open the matching page.
**Mark all as read** clears the number.

On a phone, open the menu with the ☰ button at the top left.

### Who is away and celebrations

**Who is away?** shows colleagues on approved vacation or working from home today and in the next 7 days.
Reasons are never shown. **Let's celebrate** lists birthdays (only for people who switched this on in their
profile), work anniversaries and new colleagues.

## Team calendar

**Team calendar** shows a month with vacation, working from home, events and birthdays. Choose a department to
see only your team. **Add to calendar (.ics)** downloads the month for Outlook, Thunderbird or your phone.
On a phone the calendar is shown as a list.

## Help & FAQ

**Help & FAQ** answers common questions. Search for a word or choose an area, open a question, and tell us
whether the answer helped. HR adds and edits questions directly on this page.

## Requests

### Making a request

1. Click **Requests → New request**, or use a quick action on the dashboard.
2. Choose the kind of request. The form only asks for what is needed.
3. Fill in the fields. Fields marked *(optional)* can stay empty.
4. Click **Continue to review**. Check your details.
5. Click **Send request**. You will see a confirmation.

If something is missing, a short red message appears directly below the field, for example
*"Please select a start date."* Correct it and continue.

Not ready yet? Click **Save as draft**. Drafts are only visible to you. You can finish and send them later.

### Kinds of requests

| Request | What you enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Vacation** | First and last day, an optional reason. OfficeHub counts the working days (Monday to Friday) for you. Public holidays are not deducted automatically. |
| **Home office** | The day or days you want to work from home. |
| **Equipment** | What you need (for example a laptop or a chair), a category and, if it matters, the date you need it by. |
| **Expense** | What it was for, the amount, the currency, the date and a category. You can attach the receipt (PDF, PNG or JPG). |
| **IT access** | The system or program and the access you need (view, edit or manage). |
| **General request** | A title and a short description for anything else. |

### Following your request

Open **Requests** to see all your requests. Each one shows a coloured status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** (grey) | Not sent yet. Only you can see it. |
| **Submitted** (blue) | Sent and waiting for a decision. |
| **In review** (amber) | Your manager or HR is looking at it. |
| **Approved** (green) | Approved. A comment from the person who decided may be shown. |
| **Rejected** (red) | Not approved. The reason is shown on the request. |
| **Cancelled** (grey) | You withdrew the request. |

Click a request to see all details. Under **Comments** you can ask a question or add information.
Under **History** you can see who did what and when.

### Cancelling a request

While a request is still *Submitted* or *In review*, open it and click **Cancel request**.
OfficeHub asks you to confirm, because this cannot be undone.

## Documents

Open **Documents** to find policies, handbooks, guides and templates.

- **Search**: type a word into the search box. OfficeHub searches titles **and the text inside documents**,
  so you can search for things like "vacation days" or "password".
- **Filter**: choose a category, for example *Policy* or *Template*.
- **Read**: click a document to read it directly in the browser (text and Markdown documents) or to open the PDF.
- **Download**: click the download symbol or the **Download** button.

Each document shows the date it was last updated.

### Documents for you

The **For you** tab holds files the HR team shared only with you, such as payslips or certificates.
Nobody else can see them. New files are marked **New** and you get a notification.

---

## Announcements

Open **Announcements** to read all current news. Pinned messages are always at the top.
Events show their date. Old announcements disappear automatically when they expire.

Some announcements are marked **Please read**. Click **I have read this** to confirm – HR can see who has
confirmed. Polls under an announcement show the results after you have voted; you can change your vote.

---

## People

Open **People** to find colleagues.

- **List**: search by name, department, job title or phone number. Click a number to call, or an email address to write.
- **Org chart**: see who reports to whom. Click a person to show or hide their team.
- **Phone list**: a compact table you can print or download as a CSV file.

You decide in your profile whether your phone and mobile numbers are shown to others.

---

## Rooms, desks and parking

Choose **Meeting rooms**, **Desks** or **Parking** at the top.
Desks and parking spaces are booked with one click for the **morning**, **afternoon** or the **whole day**;
you can hold one desk and one parking space at a time.

### Meeting rooms

Open **Rooms** to book a meeting room.

1. Choose the day with the arrows or the date field.
2. The bar under each room shows when it is already booked.
3. Click **Book**, enter what the room is for and choose the start and end time (quarter hours, 07:00–20:00).

Your next bookings are listed at the bottom. You can cancel them there.

---

## Visitors

Expecting a guest? Open **Visitors → Register a visitor** and enter the name, company, date, time and who they are visiting.
Reception sees all guests of the day and marks them as arrived and left.

---

## Appointment requests

Want to talk to HR, IT or your manager? Choose **New request → Appointment**, pick who you want to talk to and suggest
up to three times. When the request is approved, the chosen time is shown on the request.

---

## Employee reviews

Once a year HR sets up your employee review.

1. Open **Employee reviews** and answer the preparation questions. Click **Save for later** or **Finish preparation**.
2. Once you and your reviewer have both finished, you can read each other's preparation.
3. After the meeting the reviewer writes down the agreed goals. Read them and click **Confirm goals**.

Only you, your reviewer and HR can see your review.

## Time tracking

Open **Time tracking** and click **Clock in** when you start and **Clock out** when you finish.
The week overview shows each day, your target time and your flexitime balance. Forgot to clock in?
Add or correct an entry yourself – changes are noted. Public holidays count as days off.

Before clocking in you can choose the **project** you work on; projects you are planned for today are at the top.

**Month:** the *Month* tab shows the whole month as a calendar, one row per calendar week. Each day shows the
recorded time against the target time, days off, open requests and hints such as a missing break or a day without
any time. At the top you see your flexitime balance, the month's target and the vacation days left. Click a day to
open that week. When the month is complete, click **Hand in month**. Your manager approves it or sends it back with a
comment; while it is handed in you cannot change it. **Timesheet** downloads the month as a CSV file.

Managers find handed-in months under **Time tracking → Team → Months to approve**.

## Spaces

**Spaces** are shared areas for a team or a project. Open **Spaces** in the menu, click a space or create a new one
with **New space** (you become its manager). Each space has these tabs:

- **Overview**: recently changed files, open tasks, wiki pages and members.
- **Files**: folders and files of the space. Drag files onto the list or click **Upload**. Uploading a file with the
  same name again keeps the old one as an earlier version. **New** creates a folder, a text document, a spreadsheet,
  a presentation or a note. The clock button shows all versions; you can download or restore any of them.
  Deleted files stay in the **Trash** for 30 days.
- **Tasks**: a board with columns such as *To do*, *In progress* and *Done*. Add a card, open it to write a
  description, set a due date, a colour and who is responsible, and comment. Drag cards between columns, or open a
  card and choose the column. **My tasks** in the menu lists every card you are responsible for.
- **Chat**: talk with everyone in the space. Type **@** to mention someone – they get a notification. The paper clip
  shares a file (it is saved in the space's files). **Chat** in the menu also has **direct messages** with one person.
- **Wiki**: pages for things everyone should know. Click **Edit**, write (with a preview next to it) and **Save**.
  If a colleague is editing the same page you see their name; if they saved in the meantime, OfficeHub asks which
  version should stay, so nothing is lost. **History** shows and restores earlier versions.
- **Members**: managers add people, make them managers or remove them, change name and colour, link a project from
  planning, and archive the space when it is finished (it stays readable).

### Editing Word, Excel and PowerPoint files

If your company has set up an office server, click a document (or the pencil) to edit it right in the browser.
Several people can work in the same file at once; every save becomes a new version. If both Collabora and
ONLYOFFICE are available, you can switch between them at the top. Without an office server, files can still be
downloaded and uploaded again.

## Meetings and calls

If your company has set up its meeting server, **Meetings** appears in the menu. Meetings run on the company's own
server; nothing is recorded.

- **Plan a meeting**: give it a title, day and time, and pick who is joining. Everyone invited gets a notification
  and a calendar invitation by e-mail. Choose a space to save the notes in its wiki later.
- **Accept or decline** on the meeting page or in the list. **Add to my calendar** downloads the appointment.
- **Meet now** starts a meeting right away – copy the guest link or invite people from the meeting.
- **Join**: first you see yourself and can switch camera and microphone on or off. Click **Join now**.
- In the meeting: **Mute**, **Camera off**, **Share screen**, **Raise hand**, a **chat** just for this meeting,
  **People** (who is there) and **Notes** that everyone from the company can write together. Keys: **M** for the
  microphone, **V** for the camera.

### Calls from the chat

Click **Call** at the top of a direct conversation – your colleague's portal rings and they can accept or decline.
In a space chat, **Start a meeting** posts a "Join" message for everyone. Missed calls show up as a notification.
Every space also has a **Meeting room** at the top that anyone in the space can join at any time.

### Guests from outside

Tick **Allow guests from outside** when planning a meeting and send them the **guest link**. A guest enters a name
and waits; someone in the meeting sees "… would like to join" and clicks **Let in** or **Decline**.

## Planning

**Planning** shows who works on which project and when: one row per person, one column per day, projects as
coloured bars. Weekends, public holidays and vacation are shaded. Next to each name you see how much of the working
time is planned (for example *85 %*); days with more planned than working time get an orange line.

Everyone sees their own plan. Managers plan their team, HR everyone:

- Drag on an empty part of a row to plan someone, or click **Plan someone**.
- Drag a bar to move it, drag its left or right edge to change the dates. Click a bar to change the hours or remove it.
- **Projects** lists all projects with budget, planned and booked hours. Open a project to compare planned and booked
  hours per week and per person.

The person gets a notification when they are planned for a project.

## Surveys

Under **Surveys** you answer short questionnaires. Answers are anonymous: OfficeHub only stores that you
took part, not what you answered. Results are shown only once at least five people have answered.

## Ideas box

Share an idea under **Ideas**, support ideas of others with **Support** and follow the status
(New, Under review, Planned, Done or Not possible).

## Trainings

**Trainings** lists your trainings and certificates with the date they are due again.
The home page reminds you when a training is due soon.

## Onboarding and leaving

New colleagues see their onboarding checklist on the dashboard. HR, IT and the manager tick off their tasks;
the progress bar shows how far along everything is.

When someone leaves, HR starts a **Leaving** checklist (tab *Leaving* under Onboarding) with the last working day.
It includes returning every item of equipment the person still has; ticking it off marks the item as returned.

## My profile

Open **Profile** to check and update:

- First and last name
- Department and job title
- Office location
- Phone and mobile number, and whether colleagues can see them
- Preferred language (Deutsch or English)

Under **My quick actions** you can pin up to two quick actions to the front of your dashboard.

Click **Save**. Your email address and role are managed by the HR team.

Under **Photo** you can upload a profile photo (JPG or PNG) or remove it.
Under **Deputy** you can choose a colleague who handles your approvals while you are away, until a date you pick.
Under **Notifications** you can also get notifications by e-mail (if your company has set up e-mail).
Under **Security** you can switch on two-step sign-in: scan the code with an authenticator app, enter the code
and keep the recovery codes in a safe place.
Under **My equipment** you see the devices handed out to you.

Under **Appearance** you can choose a light or dark design.
Under **Change password** you can set a new password (at least 8 characters).

## For managers and HR

People with the *Manager* or *HR / Admin* role see an extra **Management** section in the menu.

### Approvals

- **Approvals** lists all requests waiting for a decision. Use **Filters** to narrow the list by type, status, person or date.
- Open a request and click **Approve** or **Reject**. You can add a comment for the employee – for rejections, please explain why.
- **Start review** shows the employee that you are looking at the request.
- You cannot decide on your own requests; another manager or HR does that.
- If a colleague made you their deputy, their requests also appear here until the date they set.

### Administration

- **Overview**: open requests, decisions this month, average handling time and approved vacation days this year.
- **Export approved requests**: choose a type and dates if you like, then click **Download CSV**. The file opens in Excel or LibreOffice.
- **People** (HR / Admin): add a person with a starting password, change roles or deactivate accounts.

### Reports

**Reports** offers ready-made lists: absences in a month, expenses by category, requests waiting too long and
equipment handed out. Each list can be downloaded as a CSV file.

### Portal settings (HR / Admin)

Under **Administration**:

- **Branding**: company name, logo and accent colour (OfficeHub checks that text stays readable).
- **Public holidays**: choose the country and region, and add company holidays such as a closing day.
- **Office editing**: which office server opens documents when both Collabora and ONLYOFFICE are set up.
- **Security**: require two-step sign-in for managers or HR, reset it for a person, send a test e-mail.
- **Audit log** (menu): who changed what and when (sign-ins, roles, decisions, settings); download as CSV.
- **Equipment** (menu): hand out laptops, phones or keys and see who has what.
- **People**: set each person's manager (this builds the org chart), reset a password, add people (a new person automatically gets an onboarding checklist).
- **Portal settings**: choose the quick actions on everyone's dashboard and their order, and a contact person for each section.
- **More tools (links)**: add the external applications shown in the menu.
- **Meeting rooms**: add or edit bookable rooms.

### Manage announcements (HR / Admin)

1. Click **New announcement**.
2. Enter a title, the text and a category. For events, choose the event date.
3. Tick **Pin to top** for important messages.
4. Under **More settings** you can choose a later publish date or an expiry date.
5. Click **Publish**.

Use **Edit** to change an announcement and **Archive** to hide it. Archived announcements can be restored.

### Manage documents (HR / Admin)

1. Click **Upload document** and choose a PDF, Word (.docx), Markdown (.md) or text (.txt) file.
2. Enter a title, choose a category and, if helpful, a short description.
3. Click **Upload document**.

To publish a new version, click **Edit** and choose a new file. **Archive** hides a document from employees; you can restore it later.
